# McLeod Alpha Research Report: 2025-05-20 Trading Room

## Executive Assessment

This was a choppy, two-way session in which patience distinguished the cleanest
trade from the unresolved positions. A real May 23 595-call entry at `3.19`
and a parallel 20-contract model entry at `3.17` were taken before the lower
OMG area had clearly recovered; the speaker later acknowledged jumping the
gun. Both remained unresolved near recording end. A downside OMG in 593 puts
entered at `4.12`, but strong support repeatedly blocked its `4.37` target and
the position also remained open.

The best completed trade waited for a full ABCD retracement into support: May
23 593 calls entered at `3.86` and were sold at `4.00` when price struggled at
the next Fibonacci level. The source later noted the original `4.13` target
would have filled, but accepting 14 cents was consistent with the observed
chop.

The strongest lesson is not merely "be patient." It is operational: require the
support test and recovery to finish before entry, and do not carry an
unresolved intraday OMG overnight merely because its historical win rate is
high.

## Entry and Exit Lessons

1. Complete the support test before entering the recovery.
2. Structural room must end before support, not beyond it.
3. In chop, accepting the first resistance is defensible even if the original
   target later trades.
4. Historical setup frequency does not replace a same-day time stop.
5. Parallel real/model positions can duplicate risk and attention.

## Contradictions and Risks
