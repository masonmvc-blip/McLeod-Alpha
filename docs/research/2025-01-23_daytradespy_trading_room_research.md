- The presenter described a prior tight-range trade that did not reach its 6% target, reversed, and was cut at an approximate 25% loss. The source gave no verifiable contract, timestamp, fill, or market-data record for that outcome.
- The session identified `606` calls as the pick of the day and indicated that the pick was distributed at the beginning of the session. The recording does not establish the entry, exit, target hit, or P&L for that pick.

## Reusable Research Observations

1. Test `RANGE_WIDTH_AFTER_FAILURE`: compare tight and widened opening ranges while controlling for volatility, breakout distance, and adverse excursion.
2. Test `EMA_ALIGNMENT_AND_RUNWAY` as separate admission fields. A range close, moving-average alignment, resistance clearance, and distance to the next obstacle should each be observable.
3. Treat `SIX_PERCENT_TARGET_WITH_TWENTY_FIVE_PERCENT_LOSS` as a risk-asymmetry research case, not proof of a general loss rate. The source report requires independent execution reconstruction.
4. Separate `STOP_REFERENCE` from `NEXT_DAY_HOLD`. A 40% stop suggestion and willingness to let next-Friday options sit are different and potentially conflicting management paths.

## Evidence Limitations

- The reported 25% loss and all pick-of-the-day details are source statements only.
- The recording ends before confirming the result of the `606` call pick or any next-day hold.

## Decision

No live range, EMA, runway, target, stop, sizing, or overnight-hold change is authorized. The recording supports replay research into range width, multi-timeframe confirmation, and the realized risk asymmetry between stated targets and stop/hold choices.
