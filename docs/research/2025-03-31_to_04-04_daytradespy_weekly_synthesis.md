## Ranked Falsifiable Replay Agenda

1. **Accepted Breakout vs First Penetration**: Require close through a structural level plus retest/hold, comparing it with first-touch entries. Segment by opening/later session and downtrend/uptrend state. Reject if out-of-sample expectancy, drawdown, or trade-count quality does not improve after costs.
2. **Structural Room-to-Target Gate**: Quantify distance to the next pivot, moving-average cluster, retracement, or prior extreme in underlying and option-return units. Reject if room features do not improve target attainment or risk-adjusted outcomes.
3. **Target Lifecycle Discipline**: Compare predeclared structural targets with cancelled, revised, or held positions using actual order history, fills, MFE/MAE, and costs. Reject if a predeclared target does not improve execution quality or increases missed-opportunity cost materially.
4. **Support-Failure / Reclaim Transition**: Test whether an accepted support failure or reclaim sequence predicts better continuation than a raw break or undercut. Use negative controls with shuffled levels and comparable quiet periods.
