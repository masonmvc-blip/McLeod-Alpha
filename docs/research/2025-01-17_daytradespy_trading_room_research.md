# McLeod Alpha Research Report: January 17, 2025 Trading Room

## Scope and Evidence

This report is based on a complete authorized Vimeo transcript review from `00:00` through `01:18:53`. It documents source-reported technical levels, option transactions, profit-taking rationale, and weekend management. Charts, option quotes, broker fills, and ledger evidence have not been independently reconciled.

## Later `597` Call Management

- The session later discussed `597` calls, including a source-reported market around `4.18–4.20` near the end of the recording. The presenter sought breakeven because they would not remain available to monitor the position.

## Research Implications

1. Test `EXTENDED_MOVE_PROFIT_TAKING` separately from a new directional signal. A discretionary open liquidation after a run-up cannot be scored as an OMG trade without entry context and price data.
2. Test `BREAKEVEN_SEEKING_BEFORE_ABSENCE` against predefined stop and time-based exit alternatives. The recording explicitly makes monitoring availability part of the management decision.
3. Track `WEEKEND_HOLD_CONTINGENCY` with expiry, theta, gap risk, and a concrete Tuesday action threshold. The transcript provides no quantified adverse-risk limit.
4. Retain `SOURCE_REPORTED_TARGET_REACHED` as distinct from a verified fill; the final `4.54` statement arrives after the verbal sign-off and needs independent timestamp/quote reconciliation.

## Evidence Limitations

- Reported entry prices, sale prices, contract counts, and profit calculations are not supported by broker records.
- No evidence establishes the actual result of any weekend-held position beyond the presenter’s final stated breakeven cue.

## Decision

No live entry, exit, profit-taking, breakeven, sizing, or weekend-hold policy is authorized. The recording supports replay research into post-run-up exits and time-unavailable position handling only with independent execution and risk data.
