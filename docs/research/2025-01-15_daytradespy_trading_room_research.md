# McLeod Alpha Research Report: January 15, 2025 Trading Room

## Scope and Evidence

This report is based on a complete authorized Vimeo transcript review from `00:00` through `01:09:45`. It captures source-reported CPI context, support/resistance framing, option instructions, and the presenter’s stated calculations. Underlying charts, broker executions, option-chain data, and account records were not independently reviewed.

## Market Context and Opening Framework

- The room described the CPI release as satisfactory, emphasizing a reported core month-over-month result below expectation. That macro interpretation preceded, but did not prove, the observed upside bias.
- The presenter reported an early high near `591.78`, then mapped a retracement branch through the five-minute 10 EMA, pivot R2, and `590` support. The stated downside OMG boundary was `589.90`; the first five-minute close outside the defined range was intended to select direction for a 6% option target.
- The market map was explicitly two-sided: hold the `590`/10-EMA/pivot cluster for renewed upside, or break it for a downside continuation. This is more falsifiable than a generic bullish CPI interpretation.

## Source-Reported Position Management

- Early in the recording, the presenter instructed exits for pre-existing `584` and `585` calls and referred to a `7.59` exit reference. The transcript calculates a net `826` profit on seven `584` calls using a `6.40` purchase reference and commissions; these figures are source-reported only.
- The source later stated that the OMG option target was `6.56` and that it was reached at `10:18`. The recording also referred to a separate pick-of-the-day target of `3.53`. Neither result is reconciled to a broker fill, quote timestamp, or market-data record.

## Intraday `593` Call Scalp

- After the stated upside continuation, the presenter chose January 17 `593` calls, reported a fill at `2.98`, and calculated 16 contracts from a roughly `$5,000` allocation.
- The reported exit was a `3.12` limit, derived from the entry plus the stated per-contract amount needed for a `$200` objective and commission. The presenter said the order filled, calculated `$208` net profit, and characterized it as an entry at `10:21` and exit at `10:22`.
- This is a detailed source assertion, not independent evidence that the fill, slippage, timing, liquidity, or realized P&L can be reproduced. The one-minute holding interval especially requires quote-level validation before it is used as a research result.

## Reusable Research Observations

1. Test `CPI_SUPPORT_BRANCH`: compare the `590` support/10-EMA/pivot-hold branch with the `589.90` breakdown branch rather than assigning trade direction from the inflation headline.
2. Test `EXISTING_CALL_EXIT_BEFORE_NEW_SETUP`: retain legacy-position disposition separately from the later new trade to avoid conflating portfolio repair with setup performance.
3. Test `FIXED_DOLLAR_TARGET_SCALP` only with contemporaneous bid/ask, fill probability, commissions, contract count, and latency. The stated `$200` target and 16-contract sizing create a directly measurable execution hypothesis.
4. Record `SOURCE_REPORTED_TARGET_HIT` separately from verified execution. The transcript contains distinct `6.56`, `3.53`, and `3.12` targets that refer to different trade contexts.

## Evidence Limitations

- The recording provides no independent order confirmations, quote history, or account statements for the reported trades.
- Macro figures, option prices, target hits, and profit calculations are preserved as presenter statements only.

## Decision

No live entry, exit, stop, target, sizing, expiry, or fixed-dollar-profit rule is authorized. The evidence supports controlled replay research of CPI-conditioned support, legacy-position separation, and fill-aware short-duration option targeting.
