# McLeod Alpha Research Report: January 2, 2025 Trading Room

## Scope and Evidence

This report is based on a complete authorized Vimeo transcript review, from `00:02` through `01:09:51` of a `01:10:09` recording. The raw transcript is not retained. The review establishes the room's stated market framework and trade-management discussion, but not chart-verified prices, option fills, contract selection, broker execution, or realized P&L.

## Market Context

- The room opened the first trading session of 2025 with index futures, oil, and gold described as higher. The presenter framed the backdrop against the absence of a conventional Santa Claus rally, rather than treating the futures indication as a standalone trade signal.
- The early discussion used the prior range and a stated `589` support reference to organize the session. That level was a condition to watch, not evidence by itself that a reversal or continuation was valid.

## Setups and Decision Logic

- The presenter described the OMG direction gate as the first five-minute candle closing outside the defined support/resistance lines. The stated purpose was to defer directional participation until an opening-range boundary had actually been resolved.
- A `6%` profit target was stated for the OMG setup. The transcript does not provide enough independently measurable option information to judge whether that target was executable, appropriate for the available liquidity, or consistently reached.
- The room repeatedly treated a touch of the marked area differently from a usable break. The actionable premise was a close outside the range, not anticipation ahead of confirmation.

## Trade Management and Outcome Discussion

- Late in the session, the presenter discussed holding next-week options overnight while planning to exit the current-week position by late morning or the afternoon. This is a source-described management intention, not a verified position or fill.
- The presenter said they did not expect to repair the losing position unless an unexpected upside move developed, noted no anticipated news catalyst, and characterized cutting the loss as the preferred reset for the new year.
- That sequence is a useful counterexample to treating a setup label or a stated target as a complete management plan: the later decision was driven by the absence of the expected follow-through and the remaining time/option-risk profile, not by the original setup description alone.

## Reusable Research Observations

1. Test `OPENING_RANGE_CLOSE_THROUGH` by separating first penetration, first five-minute close outside the range, and the subsequent retest or hold. The transcript supports the distinction but does not establish its expectancy.
2. Record `STRUCTURAL_SUPPORT_PROXIMITY` and `FOLLOW_THROUGH_ABSENT` together. A support reference and a breakout rule are insufficient without evidence that price accepted the break and retained room.
3. Test management outcomes separately from admission logic. The source describes an intention to avoid repairing a losing position when no catalyst or upside extension was expected; replay requires contract, bid, ask, mark, expiry, position size, and timestamps before that can be evaluated.
4. Treat stated percentage targets as observations, not deployable parameters. Compare target attainment, adverse excursion, spreads, and time-to-expiry against a baseline before inferring an exit rule.

## Evidence Limitations

- No visual chart review, synchronized underlying bars, option bid/ask/mark series, contract identifier, fill history, or canonical ledger mapping was available.
- No claimed outcome, stop quality, option premium result, or target attainment is asserted from the transcript alone.

## Decision

No live entry, exit, stop, sizing, direction, or other trading-policy change is authorized. The session supports research into opening-range close-through, follow-through failure, and time-sensitive option-loss management only after independent replay and out-of-sample validation.
