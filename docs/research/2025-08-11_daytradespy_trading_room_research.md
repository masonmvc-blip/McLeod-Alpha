## Entry and Exit Lessons

1. Preserve five-minute boundary close and one-minute confirmation as distinct.
2. Opposite-direction trades require portfolio-level exposure controls.
3. Near-target observations cannot be scored as completed exits.
4. End-of-day intent must be reconciled to an actual terminal fill.
5. Low-news summer chop favors restraint over adding correlated positions.
