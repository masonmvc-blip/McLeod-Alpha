## Definition of Done for One Recording

A recording is complete only when the evidence supports all of the following:

1. Exact post ID, source URL, title, publication date, duration, and review
   date are retained.
2. The timestamped transcript is reviewed through the recording end and its
   completeness is measured.
3. Visual review covers the full recording, with timestamps for chart,
   position, option-chain, and no-trade evidence.
4. The report contains:
   - executive assessment;
   - market and event context;
   - timestamped evidence timeline;
   - every presenter-reported trade and unresolved position;
   - setup admission, confirmation, structural room, invalidation, management,
     and exit logic;
   - contradictions, mistakes, missed trades, and no-trade decisions;
   - ledger, underlying-market, and option-execution reconciliation;
   - reusable lessons and no more than five falsifiable hypotheses;
   - instrumentation gaps, explicit non-changes, and governance decision.
5. Source-reported fills and results remain attributed claims unless
   independently reconciled.
6. The machine record, evidence bundle, registry status, and Markdown report
   agree and pass validation.

## Codex Batch Rule

Use batches of no more than three recordings. Large batches encourage shallow
summaries and missed evidence. A batch is not complete until every recording
has been reviewed from the actual authorized source and validated.
