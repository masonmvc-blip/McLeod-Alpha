## Definition of Done for One Recording

A recording is substantively complete only when:

1. Post ID, source URL, recording date, publication timing, asset identity,
   duration, and review date are retained.
2. The timestamped transcript is reviewed through the terminal available cue
   and coverage is measured.
3. Every presenter-reported trade, queued-but-unfilled idea, unresolved
   position, management decision, mistake, contradiction, missed trade, and
   explicit no-trade decision is classified.
4. Entry admission, confirmation, structural room, invalidation, management,
   and exit logic are extracted when the source supplies them; missing elements
   remain explicit.
5. Presenter fills and results remain attributed claims unless independently
   reconciled.
6. The report explains reusable lessons and records no more than five
   falsifiable hypotheses.
7. The Markdown report, machine record, and registry agree and validation
   passes.

Full visual review is required for Tier A, but an evidence-honest Tier C report
with near-complete captions is still a substantive research artifact and should
not be confused with a Tier E placeholder.

## Batch Rule

Process no more than three source recordings per research batch. Commit only
the reports, machine records, registry changes, and validation support required
for that batch. Do not alter live trading behavior.
