   they use the same contract and directional thesis.
2. Preserve original and revised OMG boundaries with decision timestamps;
   otherwise the setup is vulnerable to discretionary line movement.
3. Averaging down requires lot-level quantities, weighted basis, maximum
   adverse excursion, and a predeclared loss cap.
4. Resolve fill conflicts from order records rather than choosing the more
   favorable narrated price.

   deposit-to-current-equity ledger reconciles them.
