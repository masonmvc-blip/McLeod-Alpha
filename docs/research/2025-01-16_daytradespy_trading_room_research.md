## Market Context and Admission Logic

- The room attributed an early market boost to Taiwan Semiconductor’s reported quarterly profit and discussed retail sales reported below expectation. Those macro items supplied context, not an independently validated directional signal.
- The presenter identified the prior close around `592.79`, set OMG support at `592.64`, and set resistance at `594.92`. The initial posture was conditional: a support bounce was insufficient by itself; calls required a resistance break and continuing momentum.
- Later room commentary characterized the market as hovering in a zone and lacking a clear reason to move materially in either direction. That late assessment limits any claim that the opening technical map yielded a persistent trend.

## Source-Reported Trades

- The presenter queued January 24 `594` calls, reporting ten contracts filled at `4.90`. The stated `$200` objective implied a `5.10` target, or a $0.20 option-price increase. The position was still below that target during the recording and has no documented resolution here.
- Separately, the room reported an OMG January 17 `592` put fill at `2.18` at `09:50`. Applying the stated 6% target produced `2.31`; the presenter later said the puts sold at `2.32`.
- These contracts, prices, timestamps, quantities, targets, and fills are all presenter assertions. They are useful as replay fields but do not constitute independently verified execution evidence.

## Management and Risk Tension

- When asked about repairing the `594` calls, the presenter said it was not yet time to repair and suggested holding off on adding money. The position was described as near its target despite being under water relative to the stated entry.
- The presenter also said the calls could be held overnight and revisited the next day if they remained under target. This discretionary overnight choice differs from the same-day completion reported for the OMG put trade.
- The transcript therefore contains two distinct management regimes: a percentage-targeted intraday OMG trade and a longer-dated, fixed-dollar-target call position that could be held overnight. They should not be merged when assessing performance.

## Reusable Research Observations

1. Test `RANGE_BREAK_PLUS_MOMENTUM` by separating a support bounce, a resistance breach, and follow-through rather than accepting the first rebound as a call signal.
2. Test `OMG_PUT_TARGET_HIT` only with contemporaneous bid/ask and fills around the reported `2.18` entry and `2.31–2.32` target.
3. Track `FIXED_DOLLAR_TARGET_WITH_OVERNIGHT_DISCRETION` as a separate class, including expiry, time decay, adverse excursion, and the decision threshold for repair versus hold.
4. Treat `REPAIR_DEFERRED` as an observable management event, not a risk rule. The source stated no current repair but did not define a stop, maximum loss, or later repair trigger.

## Evidence Limitations

- No chart or market-data source independently verifies the stated technical levels or target reaches.

## Decision

No live entry, exit, repair, hold, overnight, target, or sizing policy is authorized. This session supports research into confirmation hierarchy, independently fill-validated OMG targets, and the distinct risk profile of discretionary overnight option holds.
