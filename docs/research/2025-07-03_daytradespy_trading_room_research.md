## Entry and Exit Lessons

1. Challenge schedule versioning worked only after an audible correction; store
   the effective budget and target with every trade.
2. Formal OMG, pre-signal scalps, and late continuation trades require separate
   cohorts even when all share one direction.
3. Favorable target slippage must remain source-reported until executable
   quotes and order events verify it.
4. Averaging down a same-day option changes the risk distribution and must be
   recorded as a new tranche, not merely a lower average.
5. A trailing stop can bound profit giveback, but bid-linked trigger and fill
   behavior require replay with spreads and slippage.
