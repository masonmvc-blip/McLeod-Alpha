# McLeod Alpha Research Report: 2025-05-13 Trading Room

## Executive Assessment

This recording is a direct study in obeying the tape instead of defending a
forecast. The source opened 20 May 16 582 puts at `3.11` because SPY looked
extended and bearish divergence suggested a reversal. SPY instead advanced
persistently. The source repeatedly acknowledged that every downside call had
been wrong, yet retained the puts because four days remained and profit taking
"should" eventually arrive. The position was still unresolved at recording
end and was later reported on May 14 near `1.85`, roughly 40% below entry.

The rule-based upside OMG provides the positive comparison. The source entered
May 16 584 calls at `4.00` one minute before the required five-minute close,
then openly acknowledged jumping the gun. For the rules-based record it reset
the entry to the `9:40` bar average of `4.07` and the target to `4.31`. The
option reached `4.33` at `9:51`. The outcome does not excuse the premature
fill; it shows that the confirmed version also worked and carried cleaner,
predefined evidence.

The best lesson is therefore: when the market invalidates a directional
expectation, stop using remaining expiration as a substitute for a stop. A
technical reason to expect a reversal is not the same as evidence that a
reversal has begun.

## Entry and Exit Lessons

1. **Require evidence of reversal, not merely extension.** Divergence and old
   resistance can persist while price continues trending.
2. **A winning premature entry is still a process error.** The five-minute
   close version reached target; jumping early added risk without demonstrated
   necessity.
3. **Expiration is not invalidation.** "Four days left" says nothing about
   whether the setup remains valid or the loss is acceptable.
4. **Close the loop when the tape disproves the thesis.** Repeated resistance
   breaks, sustained moving-average support, and absent selling were observable
   disconfirming evidence.
5. **Near-target is not an exit.** A position one cent from target can still
   become a large loss.
6. **Opaque trades teach little.** Without timestamp, instrument, entry, exit,
   and risk, unannounced successes or losses cannot improve the playbook.

## Falsifiable Replay Hypotheses

1. Compare reversal puts admitted on divergence alone with those admitted only
   after a support break, failed reclaim, and downside volume.
2. Compare pre-close OMG entries with entries after the qualifying five-minute
   close, controlling for option spread and target distance.
3. Test a mandatory exit after two successive resistance breaks against
   holding because expiration remains.
4. Measure opportunity cost when a losing countertrend position remains open
   during a persistent trend.
5. Test whether near-target trades benefit from a breakeven/partial rule rather
   than an unchanged fixed target.
