# McLeod Alpha Research Report: January 27, 2025 Trading Room

## Scope and Evidence

This report is based on a complete authorized Vimeo transcript review from `00:00` through `01:11:03`. It records the presenter’s stated reversal lesson, momentum framing, carried call positions, and aggregate target plan. Charts, option volatility data, executions, and account records were not independently verified.

## Market and Setup Context

- The presenter described a market reversal and referred to it as a repeated lesson, then observed renewed upside momentum in a one-minute range of approximately $0.86. The source expressed confidence in a return higher, but that was an intraday assessment rather than a validated forecast.
- The room used support/resistance and OMG framing while also referring to call-side volatility having declined from a previously high level. No implied-volatility series or option-chain evidence was provided.
- Further upside was tied to continuation through resistance; the transcript therefore does not support treating the directional confidence statement as a complete admission rule.

## Carried Position Management

- At the close of the recording, the presenter stated they were in two call positions, each described as a `$200` position. The intended disposition was to wait for price to reach a marked white range and then sell, seeking at least `$400` net across both positions.
- If that zone was not reached, the stated fallback was to retain both positions until the next day. Contract strikes, entries, expiries, stop levels, and actual P&L for the two positions are not established in the accessible transcript.
- The source’s aggregation of two positions into one `$400` objective makes it unclear whether each position had an independent risk limit or whether one trade could be held to compensate for the other.

## Reusable Research Observations

1. Test `REVERSAL_LESSON_TO_ADMISSION_RULE`: translate retrospective commentary about a crash/reversal into objectively measurable failure and recovery conditions before using it in research.
2. Track `TWO_POSITION_AGGREGATE_TARGET` with per-position entry, risk, target, expiry, correlation, and disposition. Aggregate P&L goals can obscure an individual losing position.
3. Test `VOLATILITY_CONTEXT` only from independent implied-volatility and spread data; the source claim about lower call volatility is not sufficient evidence.
4. Treat `NEXT_DAY_HOLD_AFTER_TARGET_MISS` as a distinct management state requiring adverse-excursion and gap-risk measurement.

## Evidence Limitations

- The carried positions and `$400` objective are source-reported and unreconciled.
- The recording ends before either the target zone or next-day fallback is resolved.

## Decision

No live momentum, volatility, target aggregation, holding, or sizing policy is authorized. The session supports research into per-position accountability when a presenter manages multiple options under a combined dollar objective.
