## Research Decisions

1. Treat high implied volatility, bid/ask spread, and liquidity as instrumented
   research variables, not narrative filters; require timestamped data and
   cost-adjusted comparisons.
2. Define OMG admission as an observable sequence: line state, close, retest,
   hold/fail, and structural room. Reject vague pattern narration.
3. Preserve option lifecycle evidence in separate fields for intent, order,
   target, reported sale, and verified fill.

5. Retain the research-only boundary. No entry, exit, sizing, stop, direction,

## Ranked Falsifiable Replay Agenda

1. **Volatility-and-Spread Admission Gate**: Compare acceptance only below
   predeclared implied-volatility and spread thresholds with all eligible
   signals. Reject if out-of-sample, cost-adjusted expectancy or drawdown does
   not improve.
2. **Close-Retest-Hold vs First Penetration**: Compare confirmed structural
   breaks with first-touch/first-break entries, segmented by trend and time of
   day. Reject if confirmation degrades risk-adjusted results or reduces valid
   trade count without offsetting quality.
3. **Room-to-Structure Target Gate**: Measure distance to the next pivot,
   retracement, prior extreme, or moving-average cluster in underlying and
   option-return units. Reject if room fails to improve target attainment and
   realized risk-adjusted outcome.
4. **Target Lifecycle Quality**: Reconcile predeclared target, submitted order,
   broker fill, cancellation, MFE/MAE, and costs. Reject if predeclared target
   discipline does not improve execution versus an unbiased baseline.
5. **Source-Lineage Integrity Check**: Flag duplicated asset identifiers across
   dates and measure whether deduplication alters claim-frequency estimates.
   Reject any process that permits date-specific claims from duplicate assets.
